Part one

Grok Bots

Sources for this part: docs.x.ai grok-bot pages, x.ai bot guides, grokbot.dev, and the public write-ups of people running fleets.

It feels like texting a colleague.

The colleague keeps a real, persistent desktop.

x.ai bot guide, Grok Bot 101. It can do what you can: use apps, browse the web, write and run code. You chain specialists.

What it is

  • A BotAn AI teammate with a name, a job, and context that compounds over time.
  • A computerEvery Bot works on a persistent cloud computer. A browser, a filesystem, a terminal.
  • One computerShared by all your Bots. Cookies, sessions, and files are shared.
  • Not a draftTasks finish in your real tools, instead of as chat drafts.

docs.x.ai, Grok Bot overview and computer-and-apps.

The door

  • Two appsGrok is the chat app. Grok Bot is the agent with the computer. Different apps.
  • AccessEvery paid individual Cursor plan, Cursor Teams, or a linked SuperGrok plan.
  • WhereA desktop app on macOS, Windows, and Linux. A companion app on iOS and Android.

cursor.com/help/grok-bot and docs.x.ai/grok-bot, get-started.

Standing jobs, not questions

  • The filterA job goes to a bot when it happens on a schedule, produces something you check later, and does not need your laptop.
  • One solopreneur8 agents running 11 routines across 15+ tools.
  • One operatorAbout 30 agents. A chief-of-staff bot, a family agent, engineering bots.
  • The makersThe Grok Bot team itself runs 14 bots for design, engineering, and daily life.

christopheralarcon.com, justinbutlion, How I AI on chatprd.ai, codemypixel.

Their setups, not our mandate. The filter is the takeaway: schedule, checkable output, no laptop needed.

Part two

Edit Video

Sources for this part: Descript help pages, auto-editor on GitHub, the Whisper-based editing pipelines, and the agent skills that drive ffmpeg.

Edit the text.

The video follows.

Descript's own words. Change the transcript, and the underlying media updates automatically. No timeline required.

Transcript first

  • DescriptEdit video like a doc. Change the transcript, the media updates. No timeline.
  • auto-editorA command-line first pass. It cuts the dead space by audio loudness.
  • Whisper pipelinesThey remove silences, filler words, and restarts from talking-head video.
  • Word boundariesOne skill cuts on Montreal Forced Aligner boundaries, 10 to 20 ms. It keeps the laughs.

help.descript.com, WyattBlue/auto-editor, timkulbaev/ai-video-editor, cut-video.

Say it in a sentence

  • A skillEdit video in natural language. Trim, jump cuts, captions, overlays, speed. ffmpeg and Whisper underneath.
  • A pipelineAn 8-step automated pipeline built as skills. Silence, zoom, color, audio mastering, burned-in captions.
  • Both endsDescript at the surface, or ffmpeg underneath. The transcript is the interface.

Surojit16/video-editing-skill, chrislema/videoeditor.

Set it up today

  1. ffmpeg and uv. The two prerequisites. Whisper installs itself.
  2. The tool. npx skills add WyattBlue/auto-editor.
  3. The first pass. auto-editor path/to/your/video.mp4. Dead space gone.
  4. The transcript. Whisper runs on the recording. Word-level text and timing.
  5. The edit. Delete lines, re-run. The video reshuffles to match your text.

auto-editor README, the uv and ffmpeg gist, timkulbaev/ai-video-editor.

Rung 3 also takes a margin. auto-editor example.mp4 --margin 0.2s pads the cuts so the edit feels nicer. 0.2 seconds is the default.

Part three

Generate Video

Sources for this part: Google and OpenAI model pages, the Artificial Analysis leaderboard, the 2026 comparison write-ups, and real multi-model pipeline repos.

No single model does the whole job.

A pipeline has a model per step.

The field, today

  • Veo 3.18-second videos, 720p to 4K, with natively generated audio. In the Gemini API.
  • Sora 2OpenAI's flagship video and audio model. Synced dialogue, sound effects. September 2025.
  • The endThe Sora app closed April 26, 2026. The API ended September 24, 2026. No replacement named.
  • The fieldWan 3.0, Seedance, MiniMax H3, Kling 3.0, FLUX 3, grok-imagine-video.

ai.google.dev, openai.com, help.openai.com, Artificial Analysis.

Quality, priced

  • The voteBlind pairwise human preference. Video with audio, from a text prompt.
  • The topWan 3.0 at 1156. Dreamina Seedance 2.5 at 1143. MiniMax H3 at 1137.
  • The priceMiniMax H3, $4.80 a minute. Veo 3.1, $24.00 a minute.
  • The catchVeo 3.1 ranks 18th on preference Elo. One axis, not the verdict.

Artificial Analysis, AA-Video-T2V v2.0, retrieved 8 October 2026.

Elo answers "which clip do people prefer blind." It does not answer "which fits your pipeline, your price, your edit tool." Pick by the job.

Which model for which job

  • Cinematic realismSora 2 and Veo 3.1 own the cinematic lane.
  • Image to videoKling keeps showing up in the image-to-video shootouts.
  • Editing plus generationRunway Gen-4 with timeline tooling, when editing matters as much as generation.
  • All-rounder and valueVeo 3.1, prompt adherence, native audio, 4K. Kling 3.0, value to quality, multi-shot storyboards.

motionvid.ai, toolcenter.ai, frankx.ai, 2026 comparisons.

The pipeline

  1. A model writes the script.
  2. A TTS voice speaks it.
  3. An image model draws the frames.
  4. An image-to-video model animates them.
  5. BGM and assembly land the MP4.

counter-eng/ai-video-factory. LLM, TTS, AI images, img2vid, BGM, assembly.

Who wires it

  • Eleven skillsOne job each. Research, script, storyboard, voice-over, images, timeline assembly.
  • One Google lineGemini writes. Imagen draws. Veo animates. Story to movie.
  • Multi-APIOpenAI, Gemini, ElevenLabs, and Sora 2 in one orchestration.
  • LocalThe whole pipeline runs on your own GPU.

chenyujinchj/video-agent-skills, SDSmirnov/AI-Story-To-Movie, wonderstone843, counter-eng.

Same shape as part one. One bot, one job. One skill, one job. One model, one step.

The lesson

Do not marry the model.

  • The fallThe Sora app closed April 26, 2026. The API ended September 24, 2026.
  • The gapNo replacement was named. An endpoint migration, not a model-name swap.
  • The pipelineA model per step swaps one step. A single-model stack rewrites itself.

help.openai.com, aistackcurrent.com, September 24, 2026.
