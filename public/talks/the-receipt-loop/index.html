Merge

A human decision, sized by blast radius.

  • LowNo shared contract. Easy to revert. Green verify, then review.
  • OwnedOne team's code. A receipt is attached. A code owner reviews.
  • Blast radiusShared library, schema, auth, or infra. Extra reviewer, required check, staged rollout.
